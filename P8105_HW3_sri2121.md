The cleaned dataset contains 99485 rides from four months (January and
July of 2020 and 2024). Each ride has its year and month (added from the
file names), the day of the week, ride duration in minutes, bike type
(classic or electric), rider type (member or casual), and the names of
the start and end stations. Duration ranges from 1 minute to about 239
minutes.
